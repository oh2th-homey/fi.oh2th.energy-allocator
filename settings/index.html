Configure the energy meters for your house. All values are read as cumulative kWh counters (meter_power). Positive deltas only — counters that reset are handled automatically.

Grid share Flow trigger

How much the smoothed grid share must move (percentage points) before the "grid share changed" Flow trigger fires. Meters are sampled every 5 minutes and the share is smoothed over the last 6 samples (30 min); both are fixed.

Grid meter (P1)

One device. Choose the capability that counts imported energy, and optionally the one that counts exported energy (leave export empty if you have no feed-in).

Solar (PV) sources

One or more production meters. Their deltas are summed.

Battery / EV sources

One or more devices (home battery, EV, V2G). Each needs both a charge and a discharge counter. Deltas are summed across devices.
